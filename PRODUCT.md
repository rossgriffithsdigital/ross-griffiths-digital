# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small business owners, restaurant operators, non-profits, and service businesses in Northumberland County and across Canada — people who need a proper web presence but aren't looking for a faceless agency. They're budget-conscious but not cheap; they want quality and a direct relationship. They're often comparing RGD to freelancers or US-based template services. They're hiring people, not a product.

## Product Purpose

Marketing website for Ross & Griffiths Digital, a two-person web design studio based in Cobourg, Ontario. The site converts prospective clients into booked projects by communicating: you'll work directly with Connor and Quinn (no handoffs), the results will be real (not just pretty), and the price is honest. Success means a visitor from Cobourg or Calgary booking a call without hesitation.

## Brand Personality

Quality + Canadian + Personal. RGD produces genuinely good work aligned to each business's specific needs — not template clones. They're a better Canadian alternative to US agency services: community-rooted, human, and invested in clients succeeding for real. Three-word test: **craft, care, direct**.

## Anti-references

- **Generic agency templates**: Squarespace/Wix-style builds, stock photography, blue-gradient CTAs, "We deliver results" copy. Templated sameness is the exact thing RGD sells against.
- **SaaS/startup aesthetic**: Ultra-minimal dark mode, monospace type, grid-as-personality. Feels like a devtool, not two people from Cobourg who actually care about your restaurant.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** The site itself is the portfolio. A studio selling custom websites must look unmistakably custom.
2. **Human over polished.** Warmth and directness beat corporate perfection. The reader should feel like they're talking to two real people.
3. **Show, don't tell.** Let the work, names, and direct language do the convincing. No "results-driven" filler.
4. **Canadian specificity as strength.** Northumberland County isn't a liability — it's a differentiator. Lean into place, community, and proximity.
5. **Earn every element.** Nothing decorative for its own sake. If an animation or component doesn't serve the conversion or the story, cut it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Skip links, focus-visible indicators, and keyboard navigation are already in place. Maintain: proper heading hierarchy, sufficient contrast ratios (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text), all interactive elements with accessible names, reduced-motion media query support.
