Who we are

Two friends.
One mission.

R&GD is a small web design studio built on the belief that every business deserves a proper presence online. We started in our community. We work everywhere.

"We kept noticing great businesses without a proper website, or with one that didn't reflect how good they actually were. Between our skills and a lot of time in this community, we knew we could help."

Connor Ross, Co-founder
Our story

"Every client works directly with us. No handoffs, no account managers, no runaround."

We both grew up in Northumberland County. We've spent years as regulars at local shops, restaurants, and businesses across the area — places we genuinely care about and want to see thrive.

The idea for R&GD came naturally. We kept seeing the same problem everywhere. Great businesses with no digital presence, or a website that didn't do them justice. We had the skills to fix that, so we did.

We started close to home. But good work travels. We now work with businesses across Canada and beyond — and the approach is the same wherever you are: direct, honest, and built around your actual goals.

How we operate

What you can expect from every project.

Honest

We give you straight feedback, even when it's not what you were hoping to hear. If something won't work, we'll say so. You deserve advice that actually helps, not a yes-man.

Real Results

A website that looks great but doesn't drive customers isn't a good website. We build sites designed to actually work for your business — found online, converting visitors into clients.

Community-rooted

We started this because community matters to us. That care shapes how we work with every client, everywhere.

Simple

No jargon, no unnecessary complexity. We explain what we're doing and why, in plain language, every time.

Invested
